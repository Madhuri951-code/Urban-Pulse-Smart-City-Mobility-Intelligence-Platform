# Urban Pulse – Smart City Mobility Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

Urban Pulse is a Smart City Mobility Intelligence Platform developed as part of the Infosys Springboard Virtual Internship 6.0 program. The project aims to transform raw transportation, weather, and ride-hailing data into actionable insights through interactive business intelligence dashboards built using Microsoft Power BI.

The platform integrates multiple urban mobility datasets into a unified analytical environment to analyze transportation demand, traffic conditions, ride-hailing performance, weather impacts, seasonal trends, accessibility patterns, and mobility equality across different locations. By combining data engineering, analytical modeling, and visualization techniques, the project enables data-driven decision-making for smarter urban planning and transportation management.

The project follows a complete end-to-end analytics lifecycle, beginning with data collection and preprocessing, followed by data integration, ETL (Extract, Transform, Load), feature engineering, data modeling, DAX-based calculations, KPI development, dashboard design, and insight generation. The solution leverages Power Query for data transformation, Star Schema and Snowflake Schema modeling techniques for efficient reporting, and advanced Power BI visualizations for interactive exploration of urban mobility patterns.

Throughout the project, transportation, weather, and ride-hailing datasets were cleaned, transformed, and integrated to create a scalable analytical platform capable of answering key mobility-related questions. The dashboards developed as part of the project provide visibility into traffic behavior, mobility demand, vehicle utilization, operational performance, weather influences, forecasting trends, anomaly detection, seasonal variations, and transportation accessibility.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze urban mobility and transportation demand patterns.
- Understand the impact of weather conditions on transportation behavior.
- Monitor ride-hailing performance and operational efficiency.
- Identify traffic congestion and road utilization trends.
- Forecast future transportation demand and detect anomalies.
- Study seasonal and event-driven mobility patterns.
- Support data-driven decision-making for smart city planning.
- Develop interactive dashboards for transportation analytics and intelligence.

The Urban Pulse platform successfully demonstrates how business intelligence and data analytics can be applied to real-world smart city challenges. By converting large volumes of transportation-related data into meaningful visual insights, the project helps stakeholders understand mobility patterns, improve operational efficiency, anticipate future demand, and support informed urban transportation planning.
