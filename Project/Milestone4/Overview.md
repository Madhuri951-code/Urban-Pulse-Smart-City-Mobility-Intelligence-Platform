# Milestone 4: Advanced Mobility Analytics Dashboard Suite

## Overview

Milestone 4 focuses on developing advanced analytical dashboards that provide deeper insights into urban mobility patterns, demand forecasting, anomaly detection, seasonal trends, and event-driven transportation behavior. The goal of this milestone is to move beyond descriptive analytics and enable predictive and strategic decision-making for smart city transportation management.

This milestone consists of two major dashboards:

1. **Forecast & Anomaly Intelligence Dashboard**
2. **Seasonal Demand Dashboard**

Together, these dashboards help transportation planners, mobility service providers, and city administrators understand future demand patterns, identify unusual behavior, analyze seasonal variations, and evaluate the impact of special events on ride-hailing services.

---

# 1. Forecast & Anomaly Intelligence Dashboard

## Objective

The Forecast & Anomaly Intelligence Dashboard is designed to analyze historical ride demand patterns, generate future demand forecasts, and identify anomalies that may affect transportation operations.

The dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

- What is the expected future ride demand?
- How does actual demand compare with forecasted demand?
- Which time slots experience the highest transportation demand?
- Are there unusual demand patterns that require attention?
- Which days and hours generate the highest mobility demand?

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Historical Demand
- Average Daily Demand
- Forecast Demand
- Forecast Growth Percentage
- Anomaly Count

These KPIs provide a quick summary of transportation demand performance and forecasting outcomes.

---

## Dashboard Components

### Total Demand by Time Slot and Booking Status
Analyzes ride demand across Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night time slots while categorizing bookings by their completion status.

### Total Demand by Hour
Displays hourly transportation demand patterns and identifies peak operating hours.

### Actual vs Forecast Daily Demand
Compares actual ride demand with forecasted demand to evaluate forecasting accuracy and future trends.

### Average Demand by Day of Week
Analyzes demand distribution across weekdays to identify high-demand and low-demand periods.

### Interactive Filters
- Day of Week
- Month

These filters enable detailed demand analysis for specific periods.

---

## Key Insights

- Peak transportation demand occurs during specific hourly intervals.
- Forecasted demand indicates future mobility requirements.
- Demand varies significantly across weekdays.
- Anomaly detection helps identify unusual transportation patterns.
- Historical demand trends can be used for operational planning and resource allocation.

---

# 2. Seasonal Demand Dashboard

## Objective

The Seasonal Demand Dashboard analyzes transportation demand, ride revenue, vehicle utilization, cancellation behavior, and customer activity during different seasons, festivals, holidays, and special events.

The dashboard helps understand how seasonal events influence urban mobility and ride-hailing services.

---

## Dashboard Components

### Ride Revenue by Event and Vehicle Type
A treemap visualization showing revenue contribution by different vehicle types across various seasonal events and festivals.

### Booking Value vs Ride Distance Analysis
A scatter chart analyzing the relationship between booking value and ride distance for different events.

### Booking Count by Event and Vehicle Type
Examines booking volume patterns across vehicle categories during seasonal events.

### Ride Revenue by Day and Event
Tracks daily ride revenue performance across multiple seasonal events and festivals.

### Event-Based Time Slot Analysis
A tabular view showing ride activity across different time periods for each event.

### Cancellation Rate by Event
Compares ride cancellation rates across events to identify operational challenges and customer behavior patterns.

### Interactive Event Filter
Allows users to analyze transportation performance for specific festivals, holidays, and seasonal occasions including:

- Christmas
- Diwali
- Eid-ul-Adha
- Gandhi Jayanti
- Holi
- Independence Day
- Monsoon
- Monsoon Sales
- Navratri
- New Year
- Wedding Season

---

## Key Insights

- Seasonal events significantly influence ride demand and revenue generation.
- Different vehicle types perform differently during festivals and special occasions.
- Ride distance impacts booking value across various event categories.
- Certain events experience higher cancellation rates.
- Transportation demand varies considerably across seasons and celebrations.
- Event-driven mobility patterns help optimize fleet allocation and operational planning.

# Outcome

Milestone 4 successfully extends the Urban Pulse platform from descriptive analytics to predictive and seasonal intelligence.
